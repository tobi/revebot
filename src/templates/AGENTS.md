# Home operating rules

These rules apply to this home and its workspace. They do not assign a project,
profession, current task, or personality.

- Put working files and outputs in this home's `workspace/`, unless the user selects another directory.
- Preserve existing files and ask before destructive actions affecting other work.
- Do not modify any agent's `sessions/` directory: the runtime owns those records.
- Other agents share the computer, not your task. Do not change their home files without a reason authorized by the user.
- Directory-specific rules belong in that directory's `AGENTS.md`, not here.
